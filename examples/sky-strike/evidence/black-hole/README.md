# Event Horizon, controls and settings — 2026-09-11

- Settings: compacted title/language spacing; Back ends at 91% of the panel height, within the frame. Chinese, English and Japanese controls remain live Engine GUI. Settings Back and in-game Main Menu use a distinct descending synthesized `ui-back` sound; other controls retain `ui-click`.
- Balance: elite enemies receive 50% bomb damage (Boss reduction stays 70%); asteroid HP coefficient is multiplied by 0.85 before integer rounding.
- Controls: absolute pointer events set a target. The fighter approaches smoothly (base 360 logical pixels/s, hard total speed cap 480), with gravity added to movement before the cap. Keyboard movement is affected too. Release/cancel/pause/home clear the target. Narrow-screen tracking still maps to the same logical playfield.

Level 9 `event-horizon` starts with a small `black-hole` Boss already present. Direct weapon and bomb damage cannot lower its HP. It drifts slowly in the upper region. Enemies continue entering alternately from the sides and steer toward outside lanes against gravity; sparse asteroids spawn every 2.8 seconds. Nearby friendly/enemy bullets curve inward, and captured projectiles, rocks and enemies contribute mass. Purple laser can feed the singularity. Each swallowed object now contributes 30% of its former mass. Queued mass integrates continuously at at most 18 mass/s, with a linear radius curve; radius and influence never jump on absorption. The center is explicitly bounded above 40% of logical screen height (y ≤ 384); elapsed time alone gives no mass. The HUD labels accretion progress instead of suggesting that conventional damage wins.

At 360 integrated mass, growth freezes and a full two-second warning marks a circle centered on the black hole, radius 480/960 logical pixels (half the screen height). Detonation destroys object centers in this circle and applies 100 player damage there (normal respawn protection applies). Points outside this circle are safe from blast damage, including diagonal points at the same height as dangerous central points. The WGSL warning circle uses the exact same shared radius as CPU collision checks; the outward gravitational wave remains visual across the scene. Victory scores once and transitions to level 1 after the wave.

`blackHolePass.ts` uses the Engine public renderer extension interface and actual WGSL scene sampling. The sprite scene resolves into a cached render texture; one fullscreen pass applies gravitational lensing/swirl, procedural accretion disk/photon ring/event horizon, warning illumination and an expanding refractive wave. GUI draws afterwards, undistorted. Temporary targets are resized or released on exit. At native 860×1864 with 4x MSAA the additional target allocation is 32,060,800 bytes. No extra PNG master or recurring texture upload is needed; the carousel/HUD portrait is a small generated immutable RGBA sprite.
