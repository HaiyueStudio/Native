# Stage 10 — Crystal Labyrinth / 晶镜迷阵

Stage 10 has passive triangular mirror sentries and the fast-spinning six-facet KALEIDOSCOPE / 万华晶核 boss. All visuals remain on the Engine GPU canvas and Engine GUI. Stage loading, native offline resources and Chinese/English/Japanese labels include the new level; the existing stage cycle now has ten entries.

Mirror sentries rotate at 0.32 radians/s and do not shoot. The prism rotates at 1.65 radians/s and emits up to two sentries every 3.6 seconds, capped at ten live sentries. Sentries have a reflection budget of 32; the boss budget is 1,100. Player bullet and laser damage drains this budget instead of penetrating the mirror. The final budget drain is capped at the remaining budget, and overload shatters the hull once. Bombs can clear outgoing projectiles but cannot overload these reflective hulls directly.

Swept bullet contact clips the segment against the current rotating convex facets rather than a circular silhouette. Reflection uses v − 2(v·n)n, preserving speed and converting player bullets into hostile, visibly pink-white shots. Returned shots now deal a fixed 7 damage, independently of incoming damage and the remaining budget (follow-up tuning; the original evidence below predates this change), have swept player/asteroid collision, and expire or leave the field. Player laser uses the same facet geometry, stops at the surface and creates a hostile reflected beam; its initial 350 ms dashed telegraph, damage cadence, pause and shutdown are covered by the fixture. Laser paths are straight when a mirror is hit so drawing and reflection share the incoming direction.

Boss death emits 30 spinning crystal fragments in two radial speed groups (240/310 logical pixels/s). Every fragment deals exactly 90 damage; a direct collision test leaves a full-health player at 10 armor. They survive the normal boss projectile cleanup. The transition is extended to 6.5 seconds, allowing the fragments to reach the player before the next stage clears hazards. Bombs may clear fragments in their normal area. HUD warns to dodge shards, and depleted mirror facets show cracks.

Two immutable procedural facet textures (128² for triangles, 256² for the boss) add 320 KiB of source pixels without enlarging the downloadable PNG/RGBA pack. Collision and texture generation share the same polygons. No new per-frame texture uploads or DOM UI is used.
